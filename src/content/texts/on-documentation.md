An installation is a room you were in. The photograph is a rectangle someone
chose, from a position someone stood in, at an hour someone picked. Between
those two things sits everything this archive is trying to keep hold of: the
sound that does not survive a still frame, the light that changed at four in
the afternoon, the distance you had to walk before the work resolved.

This site does not pretend the gap is closed. It states the gap, and then gives
you every measurement it has.
